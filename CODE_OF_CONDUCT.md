# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in FACET UI should be able to contribute without
harassment or discrimination. Treat people with respect regardless of their
background, identity, disability, appearance, beliefs or experience.

Discuss the work and its evidence. Make feedback specific and constructive,
listen to corrections, and give others room to participate. Disagreement does
not justify insults, intimidation, sexual harassment, threats or persistent
unwanted contact. Do not disclose another person's private information without
permission.

These standards apply to repository discussions, reviews, issues and other
project spaces, and to people representing the project elsewhere.

Report a concern privately to <hello@facet-framework.com>. Describe what happened
and provide relevant links when possible. Share sensitive evidence privately.
Maintainers will protect the privacy of reporters and affected participants as
far as handling the report allows. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

Maintainers assess reports and may request a correction, issue a warning,
remove content, restrict participation temporarily or exclude a participant.
The response depends on severity, impact and repeated behavior. A participant
may explain relevant context privately through the same contact. Maintainers
must follow these standards themselves and explain moderation decisions to the
affected participants where doing so does not expose private information.
